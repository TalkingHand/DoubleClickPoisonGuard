version=1
title=Double-Click Poison Guard
description=[h1]Double-Click Poison Guard[/h1]
description=In vanilla Build 42, double-clicking an item in your inventory eats or drinks it. The game only checks the [b]main[/b] fluid of a bottle, so a water bottle with some bleach mixed in still gets drunk, even with the poison icon showing. This mod blocks the double-click when the item is harmful [b]and you can tell it is harmful[/b].
description=
description=[h2]Blocked on double-click[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*]Food you know is poisonous
description=[*]Rotten food
description=[*]Dangerous raw food (meat, fish, eggs...) that is not cooked
description=[*]Bottles with bleach, petrol, cleaning liquid, perfume, dye, acid or other poison mixed in
description=[*]Tainted water / food, when the "tainted water text" sandbox option is on
description=[/list]
description=A red text over your character tells you when a double-click was blocked. The [b]right-click menu is unchanged[/b], so you can still eat or drink it on purpose.
description=
description=[h2]No hidden info[/h2]
description=Unknown poisonous berries or mushrooms and tainted water without the sandbox text option behave exactly as in vanilla. A blocked double-click never tells you something your character does not know.
description=
description=[h2]Compatibility[/h2]
description=Client-side only. Wraps ISInventoryPane:doContextualDblClick without replacing vanilla files. Safe to add to or remove from existing saves.
description=
description=[h2]한국어[/h2]
description=인벤토리에서 더블클릭할 때 플레이어가 알 수 있는 위험한 것은 먹거나 마시지 않습니다. 독인 줄 아는 음식, 썩은 음식, 날로 먹으면 위험한 음식, 락스·휘발유·세제 등이 섞인 병, 게임이 알려주는 오염수가 해당됩니다. 바닐라는 병에 가장 많이 든 액체만 확인해서 락스가 섞인 물병도 더블클릭하면 마십니다. 모르는 독 열매·독버섯은 바닐라와 같아서 정보가 새지 않습니다. 우클릭 메뉴로는 그대로 먹을 수 있습니다.
